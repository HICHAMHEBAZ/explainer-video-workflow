# Sourcing b-roll and rights

## Rules
- Use only: footage the user provides; free-licence or public-domain material (Wikimedia Commons, NASA, public archives); stock **only if the user chose it** in the interview; screenshots of real public sources used for commentary.
- **Literal first.** The shot is the thing the line names (the person, page, place), never a generic lookalike. If nothing literal exists, the beat becomes MOTION. Never substitute stock to fill a gap.
- **Never fabricate.** A receipt is a screenshot of the real page, post or document. If you cannot get one, the beat is MOTION and the prop is clearly an illustration.
- **Rights belong to the user.** Never approve, permit or declare rights on their behalf. Social clips (YouTube, TikTok) and public web content are fetched only after the user states a rights declaration in chat (for example "non-commercial, educational"). Record their exact words next to each fetched asset. A logged-in platform route needs a separate yes.
- **No other outlet's logo** visible in a shot. Crop it out or choose another shot. Never reuse a reference creator's footage.
- Credit archive and agency footage in the card and in `credits.md`.

## Procedure
1. **Receipts first.** Capture the real page at card size, locate the quoted text, keep its bounding box for the marker or ring.
2. **Real things.** Search the allowed archives with a literal query, in the language the subject is most likely documented in. Inspect candidates (licence, author, resolution) and preview before choosing.
3. **Auto-pick.** Take the top literal candidate whose preview shows the named thing; keep two alternates in the log.
4. **Log** every asset in `runs/<run-id>/broll.md`: beat, file or URL, source, author, licence line, alternates, rights declaration (if any).
5. Show the sourced shot with its source and licence on the Gate A card.

## Picking a shot
- One idea per shot, 1–3 s each, cut on the caption chunk.
- People doing something over empty places; close-ups over wide shots; real UGC-style clips for hooks and reactions.
- Archive black-and-white footage suits history beats; credit it.
- Screens (weather, banking, news site) go full-card or in a `short` card with the field around them.

## Preparing it
- Clips: trim to the beat, extract frames at the video fps, play them back inside the card. Stills: Ken Burns (slow zoom) with edges never showing.
- Receipts: capture with the card filled (layout `top`), then push in and ring the exact figure.
