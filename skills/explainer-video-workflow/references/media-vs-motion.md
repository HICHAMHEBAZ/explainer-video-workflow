# Routing each beat: MEDIA or MOTION

- **MEDIA**: real footage, photos, maps and documents in a rounded card on a flat field, with chunked captions.
- **MOTION**: your own motion graphics (flat colour, props, diagrams) for ideas that cannot be filmed.

Decide per beat, in this order. The first matching rule wins. Write the rule number in the scene table.

1. **Beat 1 (hook).** MEDIA if a real clip of a person reacting, or of the situation itself, exists. Otherwise a MOTION hook (mascot or object plus the question).
2. **End card.** Always MOTION.
3. **Receipt beats → MEDIA.** The line leans on a source (law, report, study, headline, post, price, app screen). Show a real screenshot with a marker on the exact line. Never redraw a document that exists.
4. **Show-the-thing beats → MEDIA.** The line names something real that could be filmed or photographed.
5. **Mechanism beats → MOTION.** How or why something works and it cannot be filmed: cause and effect, money flows, compounding, "what's inside".
6. **Numbers.** One figure tied to a real scene → MEDIA with the number over the footage. Comparing, adding, splitting or tracking over time → MOTION (equation, balance, big number, timeline, dot grid).
7. **Maps.** Your home region or a diagram → MOTION. Anywhere else → MEDIA (satellite still, flat colour fill, pins and tags).
8. **Verdicts, definitions, who's who** → MOTION (✗ / ✓, document prop with branches).
9. **Rehook and connector beats** stay in the current mode and cut to a new shot. Never switch modes on a bare connector.
10. **No usable b-roll** (no rights, nothing real fits) → MOTION. Never fake MEDIA.

## Loop tag → default mode
| Tag | Default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| stakes | MEDIA | Real people and places make stakes concrete |
| question | MEDIA (or the MOTION hook) | The viewer sees the situation they are asked to guess about |
| fake | MOTION for a mechanism, MEDIA for a document | The reveal needs explaining or proof |
| rehook | current mode | Keeps the section rhythm |
| payoff | MOTION | The final model of the idea in your own style |

## Rhythm
- Work in **sections**: runs of 2–5 beats in one mode. Change mode only where the script changes section.
- For 60 s aim for about **55–65% MEDIA, 35–45% MOTION**, with at least one MOTION section in the middle so the video keeps its own identity.
- Never more than 5 MEDIA beats in a row, never more than 4 MOTION in a row.
- The field colour changes with the section, never inside one.

## Transitions
Hard cuts only, on the caption chunk change. No fades, wipes or push-ins on the frame. When the next chunk continues the same idea, keep the card and props and change only what the chunk adds. A new section = new field colour and the card rises in (0.4 s) on its first scene only. Inside a card, motion is draw-on, pop and slide.

## Frame (9:16, 1080×1920)
| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Field | One flat colour per section (cream, sea blue, chalk are good starting points) |
| Header | Brand mark one side, series label the other, around y 250 |
| Card | x 105–975, radius 40; `top` y 330–1820 (caption over the media), `low` y 640–1820 (caption above on the field), `short` y 640–1500 (documents, tweets, phone screens) |
| Caption | One chunk at a time, aligned to the reading side, top zone y 360–380 |
| Props | Stay inside the card. A prop that leaves the card is a bug. |

Adapt the exact numbers to the user's references; keep the structure (field, header, caption, one card).

## MOTION rules
- **Flat materials only**: no glow, neon, bloom, coloured shadows, gradient orbs, glassmorphism.
- **Graphic text is data** (figures, names, ✗ / ✓, short structural words), never the caption repeated.
- **Hold, move, hold**: open on the action, hold while it completes, move, end on a still hold of at least 0.4 s so an editor can cut anywhere. Build one world larger than the frame and move a camera through it.
- **Under 2 seconds**: the viewer grasps the graphic in under 2 s at phone size. One focal object per beat.
- **Time the reveal to the chunk that says it.** Never show the answer before its caption.
- Metaphor objects for mechanisms and stakes; countable units (coins, dots, tiles, people) for comparisons, not plain bars. These are starting defaults; `style-preferences.md` overrides them.

## Rotating colours
Give each video a colour story from a small queue so consecutive videos never look alike; claim washes only for MOTION beats.
