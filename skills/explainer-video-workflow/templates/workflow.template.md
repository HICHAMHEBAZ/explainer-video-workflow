## Step details
Each step follows `references/workflow-steps.md` of the skill. The essentials:

1. **Script.** About {{BEATS}} beats, {{WORDS}} spoken words. Stake or question inside the first 1.5 s. Loop tags per beat; under the table: big question, head fakes, final rehook.
2. **Route** every beat with the 10-rule list in `references/media-vs-motion.md`; aim for 55–65% MEDIA and 35–45% MOTION.
3. **Source** literal b-roll only. Rights: {{BROLL_POLICY}}. Never declare rights for the user.
4. **Gate A.** Do not build in normal mode before approval.
5. **QA** before the user sees anything: zero-token checks first.
6. **Gate B.** The user approves, edits, or sends notes.

## Hard rules
- Never invent numbers, quotes, receipts or logos.
- Never self-approve; builders never review their own scenes.
- Safe zones: no copy above y 260, below y 1480, or at x > 940 below y 1000.
- Hard cuts only. Flat materials only.
