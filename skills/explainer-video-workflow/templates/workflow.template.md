## Step details
Each step follows `references/workflow-steps.md` of the skill. The essentials:

1. **Script.** About {{BEATS}} beats, {{WORDS}} spoken words, written in your voice. One viewer, one gap; stake or question inside the first 1.5 s.
2. **Route** every beat with `references/media-vs-motion.md`: footage shows the named thing, graphics explain concepts.
3. **Scout, then source** literal b-roll only. Rights: {{BROLL_POLICY}}. Never declared for you.
4. **Voice first.** The recorded voice is the truth; captions follow it.
5. **Workers** start only when `words.json` is final; max 3 in parallel.
6. **QA** before you see anything.
7. **Export** only when you say "export".

## Hard rules
- Never invent numbers, quotes, receipts or logos.
- Never self-approve; builders never review their own scenes.
